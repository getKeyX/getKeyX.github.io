KeyX 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日

简单来说：KeyX 没有服务器，不需要注册，不收集、不出售、也不分享你的任何个人数据。

  • 你的密码库加密后保存在你的设备上。
  • 开启同步后，数据先在设备上加密，再存进你自己的 iCloud。我们和 Apple 都读不到内容。
  • App 里没有统计工具、广告或任何第三方追踪。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的 KeyX App（包括其自动填充扩展）以及本网站。KeyX 由独立开发者开发和维护，下文中的“我们”即指 KeyX 的开发者。

2. 我们不收集任何个人数据

KeyX 不运营任何服务器，也没有账号系统。我们不会收集、存储或接收你的密码、条目内容、邮箱、姓名、位置、设备标识、联系人或使用习惯。App 不包含任何统计、广告、崩溃上报或其他第三方 SDK，不会追踪你，也不会把任何数据卖给或提供给第三方。

3. 你的数据保存在哪里

在你的设备上

你在 KeyX 里保存的所有内容（密码、通行密钥、两步验证密钥、笔记、标签等）都用由你的主密码派生的密钥加密，保存在你的设备上。我们不知道你的主密码，也无法替你重置。设置主密码时 App 会给你一把恢复密钥，请妥善保管，它是你忘记主密码时找回密码库的唯一方式。

App 里的“账户”

KeyX 中可以创建的本地账户只保存在本机，用来在同一台设备上区分不同的密码库，不会上传到任何地方。

iCloud 同步（可选）

如果你开启 iCloud 同步，数据会先在设备上用 AES-256-GCM 加密，再通过 Apple 的 CloudKit 保存到你自己 iCloud 账户的私有数据库中。加密密钥不会离开你的设备，因此我们和 Apple 都无法读取其中的内容。这部分数据的存储由 Apple 提供，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

iCloud 云盘加密备份（可选）

如果你开启自动备份，KeyX 会把加密后的备份文件保存到你自己的 iCloud 云盘。没有你的主密码，备份文件无法打开。

4. 共享与紧急访问

共享保险库

共享保险库通过 Apple 的 iCloud 共享功能实现。共享内容在设备上加密，只有你邀请并接受邀请的人才能解密。参与者由你决定，你可以随时移除成员或停止共享。

紧急访问

你指定的紧急联系人可以申请只读访问你选择的保险库。申请会通知你，在你设定的等待期内你可以随时拒绝。整个过程同样通过 iCloud 完成，内容全程加密。

一次性链接

当你用一次性链接分享某个条目的部分字段时，这些内容会在设备上加密后，临时存放在 Apple 为 KeyX 提供的 iCloud 公共数据库中。解密所需的密钥只包含在你发出的链接里（你还可以额外设置口令），不会上传。链接到期后自动失效，相关记录会被删除。

5. App 使用的系统权限

  • 相机：仅用于扫描二维码（例如两步验证二维码、加入共享保险库的二维码）。画面只在设备上实时识别，不会保存或上传。
  • 面容 ID / 触控 ID：用于解锁 App，验证由系统完成，我们拿不到任何生物特征信息。
  • 通知：用于证件或会员到期提醒、共享保险库的动态，以及订阅试用即将结束的提醒。提醒由设备本地生成或通过你的 iCloud 触发。
  • 自动填充：在设备上把你选择的密码、通行密钥或验证码填入其他 App 和网站，不经过任何服务器。

以上权限都可以随时在 iOS 的“设置”中关闭。

6. 购买与订阅

KeyX Pro 的购买、订阅、续费和退款全部由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。我们收不到你的付款信息、Apple ID 或联系方式；App 只会从 Apple 读取购买是否有效，用来开启 Pro 功能。

7. Apple 可能收集的信息

如果你在 iOS 中选择了“与 App 开发者共享”分析数据，Apple 可能会向开发者提供匿名的崩溃报告和使用统计。这类信息由 Apple 按照其隐私政策收集和处理，不包含你的密码库内容。

8. 删除你的数据

  • 删除 KeyX App，即删除设备上保存的所有数据。
  • iCloud 中的同步数据和备份可以在 iOS 的“设置 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理账户储存空间”中删除。
  • 你也可以在 App 内关闭同步、停止共享或移除紧急联系人。

因为我们不保存你的任何数据，所以没有需要向我们申请删除的内容。

9. 儿童隐私

KeyX 不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

10. 政策更新

如果本政策有实质性变化，我们会更新本页面顶部的生效日期，并在 App 更新说明中告知。

11. 联系我们

如果你对本政策有任何问题，请发邮件到 solocoding@outlook.com。

KeyX Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

In short: KeyX has no servers and no sign-up. We don't collect, sell or share any of your personal data.

  • Your vault is encrypted and stored on your device.
  • If you turn on sync, data is encrypted on your device first and then stored in your own iCloud. Neither we nor Apple can read it.
  • The app contains no analytics, no ads and no third-party tracking.

1. Scope

This policy covers the KeyX app for iPhone and iPad (including its AutoFill extension) and this website. KeyX is built and maintained by an independent developer, referred to as “we” below.

2. We don't collect personal data

KeyX doesn't run any servers and has no account system. We don't collect, store or receive your passwords, item contents, email address, name, location, device identifiers, contacts or usage habits. The app includes no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs. It doesn't track you, and we never sell or give any data to third parties.

3. Where your data is stored

On your device

Everything you save in KeyX (passwords, passkeys, verification-code secrets, notes, tags and so on) is encrypted with a key derived from your master password and stored on your device. We don't know your master password and can't reset it for you. When you set it up, the app gives you a recovery key. Keep it safe: it's the only way back into your vault if you forget your master password.

Accounts inside the app

Local accounts you create in KeyX exist only on that device, to keep separate vaults apart on a shared device. They are never uploaded.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM and then stored through Apple's CloudKit in the private database of your own iCloud account. The encryption keys never leave your devices, so neither we nor Apple can read the contents. Storage is provided by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Encrypted backups to iCloud Drive (optional)

If you turn on automatic backups, KeyX saves encrypted backup files to your own iCloud Drive. They can't be opened without your master password.

4. Sharing and emergency access

Shared vaults

Shared vaults use Apple's iCloud sharing. Shared contents are encrypted on your device and can only be decrypted by people you invite who accept the invitation. You decide who takes part, and you can remove members or stop sharing at any time.

Emergency Access

An emergency contact you choose can request read-only access to a vault you pick. You're notified of the request and can decline it at any time during the waiting period you set. This also runs through iCloud, and contents stay encrypted throughout.

One-time links

When you share some fields of an item with a one-time link, they're encrypted on your device and stored temporarily in an iCloud public database that Apple provides for KeyX. The key needed to decrypt them is contained only in the link you send (you can add a passphrase too) and is never uploaded. When the link expires, it stops working and the record is deleted.

5. System permissions the app uses

  • Camera: only to scan QR codes, such as two-factor setup codes or codes for joining a shared vault. Images are processed live on your device and never saved or uploaded.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to unlock the app. The system performs the check; we never receive biometric data.
  • Notifications: for expiry reminders, shared vault activity, and a reminder before a subscription trial ends. They're created on your device or triggered through your iCloud.
  • AutoFill: fills the passwords, passkeys or codes you choose into other apps and websites, on your device, without any server.

You can turn any of these off at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases, subscriptions, renewals and refunds for KeyX Pro are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details, Apple ID or contact information. The app only checks with Apple whether a purchase is active, to turn on Pro features.

7. Information Apple may collect

If you choose “Share with App Developers” for analytics in iOS, Apple may provide developers with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple collects and handles this under its own privacy policy, and it never includes the contents of your vault.

8. Deleting your data

  • Deleting the KeyX app deletes all data stored on that device.
  • Synced data and backups in iCloud can be deleted in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.
  • Inside the app you can also turn off sync, stop sharing or remove emergency contacts.

Because we don't keep any of your data, there's nothing to ask us to delete.

9. Children's privacy

KeyX doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page and mention it in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email solocoding@outlook.com.
