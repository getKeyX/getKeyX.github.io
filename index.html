常见问题Questions

忘了主密码怎么办？What if I forget my master password?

KeyX 没有服务器，所以我们无法帮你重置主密码。设置主密码时，KeyX 会给你一把恢复密钥，请把它保存在安全的地方，这是找回密码库的唯一办法。

KeyX has no servers, so we can't reset your master password. When you set it up, KeyX gives you a recovery key. Keep it somewhere safe: it's the only way back into your vault.

不开 iCloud 同步可以用吗？Can I use KeyX without iCloud sync?

可以。不开同步时，数据只存在这一台设备上。你也可以随时在设置里开启或关闭同步。

Yes. Without sync, your data lives only on that device. You can turn sync on or off anytime in Settings.

你们能看到我的密码吗？Can you see my passwords?

不能。密码库用只有你知道的主密码加密，同步到 iCloud 之前也会先加密。我们没有服务器，从来接触不到你的数据。

No. Your vault is encrypted with a master password only you know, and it's encrypted again before it goes to iCloud. We have no servers and never touch your data.

以后想换别的密码管理器，能把数据带走吗？Can I take my data with me later?

能。你可以随时把整个密码库导出成加密备份文件，不付费也可以。

Yes. You can export your whole vault as an encrypted backup file anytime, with or without Pro.
